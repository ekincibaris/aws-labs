The invoices were uploaded successfully, and the Lambda function and the EventBridge rule were configured correctly. There was no configuration issue on the EventBridge or Lambda side, but the EventBridge rule was never triggered. So it was fairly obvious that Amazon S3 wasn't sending any events to EventBridge. I noticed that the bucket was created last week, so it was a new one, and EventBridge notifications are not on by default. So I went to check whether they were enabled, and they were disabled. When I enabled them in the console, S3 started sending events to EventBridge and then to the Lambda function, and it worked perfectly.
